About me

I'm an MSc student in the Department of Physics at Shahjalal University of Science and Technology (SUST), Sylhet, Bangladesh, where I also completed my B.Sc. in physics. I'm presently exploring Nonlinear optical properties of material and microscopy image enhancement techniques at the Nonlinear Optics Research Laboratory, Department of Physics, SUST.

My thesis work centers on the Z-scan technique to measure nonlinear refraction and absorption of materials, under the supervision of Professor Dr. Md. Enamul Hoque. Besides, I keep a running interest in image processing and phase contrast microscopy, which grew out of an undergraduate project on FFT-based microscopy image enhancement

Take a look at the Research page for the details, or the Blog for what I'm writing about.
